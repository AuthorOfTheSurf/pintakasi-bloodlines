# runs/ — the field journal's raw material

Full sim-run logs, preserved verbatim: the game playing out day by day, the
brains answering (actions + thinking time per barn), the doctor's verdicts,
the failures exactly as they printed. `BRAINS.md` tells the story; these are
the receipts. Logs are cheap and this history does not regenerate — when a
run matters, its log lands here.

- `phase4/` — the first full-fleet week (and its two famous failures), the
  30b bench, and the 7-day persona A/B arms.
- `10v10/` — the 91-day experiment: 10 scripted vs 10 llm barns, coached
  every 4 weeks (see COACHING.md). One log per segment.
