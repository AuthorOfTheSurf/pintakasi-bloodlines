# RULINGS

A ledger of WHAT was ruled and WHEN — rounds ~14 through 31. The *reasoning*
lives in the dated comment blocks in `src/engine/config.ts` (and, for a few
items, in the test files that pin the behavior); this file exists so an
agent can see a knob's history without reading that whole file first.
Append-only — when a rule is reversed or re-tuned, add a new line, don't
edit the old one. Round numbers and dates come from `git log --oneline`
(rounds 14–19 landed 2026-08-03, rounds 20–23 landed 2026-08-04).

## Round-by-round

**Round 14** (2026-08-03) — the naming law: no bird fights under an auto-name. Gacha egg-drop weights retuned (2 free pulls/day × a 23% egg rate was out-producing the breeding barn — "Mystery Egg (Blue) VIII" in the feed was the tell); ~16 eggs/100 rolls after. Gacha birds' stats CONSTRAINED so no tier's `statMax` beats the starter ceiling by more than a whisker (the old Gold tier at 350–700 strictly dominated gen-1 breeding) — the gacha's jackpot is STARS, never raw stats. Pixel-art coats added (base coat + element-tinted trim); a bred chick inherits a parent's base coat with a small mutation chance (placeholder genetics). **Paid gacha rolls start feeding the juice pool** — this closes the first of the two silent-burn incidents AGENTS.md's "GP is never printed or burned" rule calls out: gacha spend used to vanish rather than route anywhere (the second incident is `buyLand`, not fixed until round 22 — see below).

**Round 15** (2026-08-03) — cold start: every stable begins with 4 age-0 eggs, nobody fights week one. ONE lifetime record (round 9's practice/juvenile split collapses back into a single win/loss column that counts everywhere). Ages freeze at retirement. Colored letter grades (O/O+ tiers) in the Birds table. "Studding" status added.

**Round 16** (2026-08-03) — topline diffs (a snapshot per tick, day or week span) power the admin's before/after cards. THE FIGHT ECONOMY PRINCIPLE is stated here for the first time in these terms: standard fights print no GP — pure pot, win +entry / lose −entry, the subsidy is Land Tokens not GP. This is the "round 16 ruled" baseline that round 22's fight rake later breaks and round 23 restores (see Reversals table).

**Round 17** (2026-08-03) — The Card schedule view. Cancellation fixes: a per-farm lobby cap, and auto-play cards by style (shared logic with the bots, `formatScores`/`bestFormat` in `bots.ts`).

**Round 18** (2026-08-03, ruled 2026-08-03) — **THE PINTAKASI** launches: three weekly blade championships, one per "distance" (Long Knife + Short Gaff always run; the middle blade rotates Short Knife / Long Gaff by week parity). Hardcore throughout, age 3+, entry fee 200 GP (`LAND_BASIS` still carries this number today, held as "the stake the crowns represent" even though round 22 made entry free). Committee-seeded bracket (1v16, 8v9…) ranked by earnings → wins → avg figure; byes to the top seeds; the Selection Committee live-bumps a full field's weakest entrant. GP to the top (purse = the week's juice-pool share, first-round losers zeroed), land to the fallen (elimination grants pay the earliest-out the most). One bird per stable per week at launch (see round 19).

**Round 19** (2026-08-03) — demo polish. **One bird per CROWN, not one per stable** — the round-18 rule stopped after a single weekly entry, which starved a three-crown week down to one field of seven across ten farms; now a barn declares a specialist per blade. Class ladder reads STAKES wins (not lifetime wins) so maidens exist again — the discovery year doesn't graduate anybody. Player-side claiming. Starter TALENT SPIKE: each stat gets a small chance to roll above the starter band, so ~a third of birds show a green grade somewhere on day one (the six-stat average still sits in B/B+ — raising the whole bird stays breeding's job).

**Round 20** (2026-08-04) — **Pit Figure REBUILT, PFL-true.** The old figure scored each bird against its opponent, which let a loser out-figure the winner of the same fight; now the winner's figure is absolute (measured against a maxed-out ghost pace) and the loser is scored down from the winner by beaten lengths — a loser can never figure above the bird that beat it. Crown day **moves Wednesday → Thursday** (a bird gets one more ordinary card first). **MAX_PER_BARN: 1 → 3** — a deep barn may load one championship with three specialists, not one. Juveniles fight juveniles only (previously mixed into the open-age lobbies). Eggs hide their stats on the board and just read "Egg". 1 free gacha pull/day. World genesis seeds the juice pool with `SEED_JUICE` = 2,400 GP (3 days of drip) so the first championships are worth entering before breed fees fill the pool.

**Round 21** (2026-08-04) — the Staking tab: total LT staked and GP earned via staking, per barn and world-wide. This is a measurement round, not a rule change — but the numbers it exposed (ten barns, 10,627 LT staked, 55.96 GP earned across 35 days — breed fees were the pool's *only* inflow) are the direct cause of round 22's LT-flywheel ruling.

**Round 22** (2026-08-04) — **the LT flywheel**: every way GP changes hands now pays the land stakers a slice of the gross, in centi-GP. `STAKER_FLOWS`: fight pots 2% (reverses round 16 — see Reversals table), claim tags 2%, gacha spend 10%, breed fees doubled 2.5% → 5% (2.5% was round 10's original rate), land purchases 100% (previously silently burned — the second of the two silent-burn bugs AGENTS.md calls out). The Selection Committee's bump-line ranking switches to POINTS-first (previously earnings-first) — campaigning beats a fat wallet.

**Round 26** (2026-08-04) — **the big balance round.** Everything below was measured before and after by the lab; BALANCE.md carries the numbers.
- **Blades enumerated B1–B4** (keys and labels; the sabong weapons live on in `flavor`). Ordered sprint → marathon so a future fifth blade slots in and the MIDDLE blade becomes the balance anchor — Zane wants an odd blade count where the midpoint weighs every stat evenly and tuning works outward.
- **Station rebuilt: gate → slope** (`BATTLE.UNDERDOG_CLAWBACK` = 0.5 replaces `UNDERDOG_RATIO`/`STATION_DIVISOR`). The old binary gate INVERTED the grade ladder (a +100-per-stat bird lost 59–67%) and carved two one-point cliffs. Now the outmatched bird claws back station/2000 × 0.5 × the gap's per-roll value — capped below the gap (a superior same-shape bird is ALWAYS favored; Zane: "otherwise there's no point in breeding"), smooth from zero, station excluded from the totals it compares (heart, not class — counted in, the stat self-cancelled).
- **Stars reworked into the element's volume knob** (Zane's declared intent since round 25's report): both element edges scale by halfStars/10; the flat `BOOST_PER_FULL_STAR` stat boost is DELETED. 0★ = the element is a color on the card; every half-step is a real rung (the old floor threw half the ladder away). `ELEMENT_EDGE` 0.5 → **1.0** and `WEATHER.EDGE` 0.25 → **0.5**, re-read as CEILINGS — a 2.5★ bird gets exactly the old ruled values, only 5★ sees the full edge.
- **`BATTLE.ROLL_DIVISOR` 400 → 85**, tuned at B3 (the future midpoint) against Zane's grade targets: +100 on every stat ≈ 80% win, +200 ≈ 98%. B3 measures 82.9 / 97.4. The blade ends miss by ±10 — turn count amplifies stats — and that spread is the deferred phase-weight rework's job, not this knob's.
- **Condition re-ruled as intended** (the PFL-Temper theory): the boost is not a bug, condition is the wildcard stat — targets no distance, makes everything else arrive reliably, can offset a stat weakness. Comment and Handbook updated; no mechanic change.
- Figure fixes riding along: the loser is scored down from the winner's CLAMPED figure (a maiden flattened by a monster used to post 145), and floored at 0 (the round-25-recorded −5 bug).
- Doctor's weather-timing metric counts STARRED entries only (a 0★ bird has no going to play), and `weatherMatched` in bots requires stars for the same reason.
- Deferred, explicitly: the phase/blade-weight rework (waiting on the new blade lengths), Crowd Noise (station's parity role), logarithmic breeding speed, and any tuning of the 5★ stacked ceiling (95.8% on B4 with wheel + weather — watch it as the population's stars climb).

**Round 27** (2026-08-05, two commits) — **the five-blade dial and the distance rework.** Zane's PFL distance theory made mechanical; every number verified by the lab, before and after (BALANCE.md rewritten).
- **B5 added** (Needle Gaff, 45 turns, `damageMult` 2, `critMult` 1.05) — the fifth blade the round-26 enumeration was FOR. B3 (20 turns) becomes the dial's true middle.
- **The crown schedules go FIXED**: the Majors run B1/B3/B5 every week (Sprint / Middle / Classic, PFL-style; the round-18 middle-blade rotation dies), and the Juvenile Championship runs B2/B4 every week (the round-23 parity rotation dies). Between the two stages, all five blades crown somebody every single week.
- **The weight matrix replaces the absolute phase windows** (`FORMATS[].weights`). Every turn roll blends ALL FOUR distance stats, weighted per blade; one key stat per off-center blade (B1 agility=Start, B2 sight=Speed, B4 stamina, B5 gameness=Finish) and **B3 keys NOBODY** — measured even to 0.4 points, and no same-total specialist beats a flat bird there. The matrix is symmetric across the dial (agility's column mirrors gameness's, sight's mirrors stamina's) — PFL's distance symmetry as a config property, audited by the lab's `reach` case. `PHASES` survives as narration only (break/open/deep are chapters, not stat switches).
- **Wind goes UNIFORM at 100** (`BATTLE.WIND`; `BASE_WIND`/`WIND_PER_STAMINA` deleted) — Zane: "I'd make HP uniform across all birds (e.g. 100 HP), and then build the stats from there." No stat buys hit points anymore; every `damageMult` rescaled (13/7/4.5/3/2) and `GHOST_PACE` recalibrated (starters figure ~52 everywhere).
- **Stamina becomes the FUEL TANK** (`BATTLE.FUEL`: 8 base turns + 0.014/point, wall factor 0.5) — the hidden-gas theory. Past the tank the bird HITS THE WALL: agility/sight halve; stamina and gameness never fade (grit is mental). Old decay (`DECAY_PER_TURN`/`DECAY_FLOOR`) deleted — the lab had measured it decorative (≤0.3 points). Measured wall rates: 0% of B1/B2 fights, 37% B3, 85% B4, 99.5% B5. Honest split: stamina's direct weight carries most of its value; the wall adds 2–4 points on the middle blades and the "running on heart" story everywhere long.
- **`statScale` — the per-blade loudness dial** (1.5/1.15/1.0/0.9/0.8): scales everything the bird brings (blend, clawback, deep-water gameness bonus, BOTH element edges) so one grade of breeding buys the same win rate at every distance. Closes round 26's ±10-point blade-end spread: +100 now pays 76–85% on every blade, +200 pays 92–98. The one accepted miss: B1's +200 (91.8 vs 98) is a variance floor — five turns of dice cannot deliver 98%, and the sprint stays the upset blade by identity. Side effect, free: the 5★ wheel+weather stack eases 95.8% → 94.9%.
- Gameness KEEPS its morale job (the quit check and the low-wind bonus) — pure-distance-stat was considered and rejected; the run-or-die drama is sabong.
- `intent.ts` re-ruled to the round-27 map (B3 gets an `even` intent judged on spread, not order); all five blades now MATCH intent — round 26's "targets to measure against, not tune to" stance deliberately reversed, because the middle exists now.
- Bots' `formatScores` now reads the engine's own weight matrix (a weights retune re-teaches every bot for free). Figure guard: a winner never posts below one band.
- Deferred, still: Crowd Noise (station's parity role), logarithmic breeding speed, and the 5★ stacked ceiling watch.

**Round 28** (2026-08-05) — **THE FOG COMES DOWN: stats hidden until retirement.** Zane's ruling, closing the oldest contradiction in the game: the MCP prose had said "the skill is DISCOVERY" since round 13, while get_bird handed every owner the full sheet — and the bots proved the loop dead by carding perfectly from day one.
- **The six fighting stats are `null` on every live bird's view** (egg or active) and REVEAL at retirement, whatever ended the career — manual, hardcore loss, or the age cap. The reveal is announced in the retire event ("The sheet is public: <grade> overall"). Gacha pulls hidden too — round 14's "the jackpot is STARS, never raw stats" becomes literal. Stars, element, carriage, sex, age, record and figures stay visible: they are the card, not the sheet. Admin stays omniscient (it reads raw rows — the dev inspector, not a player surface).
- **The SCOUT REPORT** (`Lobbies.scoutReport`, `SCOUT` config block): per blade, the record + average/best figure + a score SHRUNK toward PRIOR_FIGURE (50, the even-starter figure) by PRIOR_WEIGHT (2) pseudo-fights — one lucky 80 must not out-rank three honest 60s, and an unraced blade reads "unknown, average", never "bad". Surfaced on get_bird; players, auto-play and bots all read THIS, identical evidence, no side channel.
- **The bots stop cheating**: `formatScores` (true stats × the weight matrix) is deleted. `bestFormat` now explores — while any blade has fewer than MIN_READS (2) figures, EXPLORE (35%) of cards go to the least-read blade, because without forced exploration a bird's first blade is self-fulfilling. Otherwise argmax scout score with JITTER (10) of judge error. Crown declarations sort on scout scores, deterministic (nobody experiments in the ring that retires losers), with round-robin spread on exact ties — a barn of unraced veterans would otherwise pile its whole roster into one crown.
- **The stud market gets the sheet**: StudView carries the revealed six stats + overall grade — every stud is retired by definition, and the revealed sheet is the sales pitch. Breeding is the INFORMED decision; fighting is the DISCOVERED one. (Grades now appear only on retired birds and studs.)
- **The doctor grows a discovery section** (omniscient by design): % of entries carded at the bird's TRUE best blade by age bucket — the answer key the players lost — plus juvenile blade coverage as exploration's adoption check.

**Round 28a** (2026-08-05) — **the overall grade is the fog's one exception, and the pairs question gets measured.** Two small rulings from Zane, one of which turned into the biggest open item in BALANCE.md.
- **`overallGrade` is ALWAYS public** — on eggs, on juveniles, on live fighters, not just on retirees and studs. Zane: "it's a small hint at the fighting strength of the bird, and it doesn't indicate how to place the bird into fights." That is exactly the split the fog is drawn along: the grade says HOW STRONG (six-stat average as a letter), never WHAT SHAPE — a B+ sprinter and a B+ stayer read identically, so it cannot answer the blade question discovery exists to ask. It also gives a fresh hatch something to be proud of on day one and the claiming ring an honest read on a bird with no figures yet. Computed in `Flock.view()` from the raw row, pinned by a test, taught in the Handbook and the MCP doctrine.
- **The scout report is confirmed as "fight history, shrunk"** — no new data, no engine internals: per blade the record and the figures the bird actually posted, plus a score pulled toward a neutral prior so a one-fight blade cannot out-rank a three-fight one. Bots read the same object a player does.
- **The `pairs` balance case** (`bun run balance pairs`, ruling PAIR_INTENT): three tables — the RANGE a pair covers, how much of the sum of its parts a pair keeps, and a shape comparison of a pair against a single spike carrying the same total surplus. **The control is always a FLAT bird.** The first cut of this case measured pair-vs-spike at equal totals (both birds carrying surplus) and warned on all six rows; Zane corrected it — that is the "which is the better way to spend 200 points" question, which he did not ask, and single-stat spikes are a fine line anyway because stars, station and condition are still on the table. Corrected, the hypothesis CONFIRMS: `[450, 450, 350, 350]` measures 70.8 / 69.2 / 68.2 / 59.0 / 56.2 across B1–B5 — two homes, a good middle, and no blade below 55% for any pair. Range costs about 5 points of peak and buys back about 5 at the second home and at B3 (spike spreads 16.7–31.8, pair spreads 9.7–25.3). **B3 turns out to be every pair's blade**: 67.9–69.4 for all six, because the even blade credits both halves of a two-stat bird while a spike collects on one — so the case exempts the even blade from its home check (read off `intent.ts`, never hardcoded). Verdicts are interval-honest: a naive top-2 check warned four times on gaps of ~1.5 points inside a ±2.1 interval.


**Round 29** (2026-08-06) — **the discovery loop measured, and the flock finally bred for shape.** Round 28 built the fog, the scout report and the doctor's discovery audit, then reported a scout accuracy near chance. Three separate things were wrong, and only one of them was the engine.
- **`SCOUT.PRIOR_FIGURE` 50 → 30, because 50 was never measured.** The comment claimed it was the figure an even fight between starters produces — round 27's uniform-100 wind rescaled every `damageMult` and the level calibration went with it. The `symmetry` control reads **26.9 / 27.1 / 29.3 / 30.2 / 31.5** and the live world's mean normalized figure is **32.1**. A prior 18 points above reality meant every blade a bird had actually fought got dragged toward 32 while every blade it hadn't stayed parked at 50: **evidence lost to ignorance, by construction.** Replaying the 5,505 entries of `sim-20260806-1318` with only this number changed moved scout accuracy **26.5% → 31.2% exact** and **52.2% → 56.0% on-or-adjacent**; `PRIOR_WEIGHT` at 0.5/1/2 all landed within 0.3, so this was the prior, not the Bayes. The proper fix — re-tuning `GHOST_PACE` so ~50 really is average, which would also stretch live figures off their current ~5–55 band and shrink `NOISE` relative to the signal — is **deferred to its own round**: it moves `GHOST_FIGURE`, `CLASS_BASE`, `MAX` and the Handbook's figure pages together, and an S+ specialist already reads 110 against a clamp at 150. The stale claim is now marked ⚠ MEASURED in `config.ts` rather than left standing.
- **Two things were tested and rejected, so nobody retries them.** *Normalizing the result out of the figure* (subtracting the win/loss means, 55.3 / 8.9) DROPPED accuracy 31.2% → 23.2%: winning at a blade IS blade-fit evidence, and stripping it strips the signal. *Weather normalization* is worth only **+0.2 to +0.8 points** — real, but not the bottleneck, because only ~25% of entries are timed and stars scale the effect. It needs no schema change whenever it is wanted (`weatherOfDay(dayIndex)` is a pure function of a column already stored).
- **The doctor's discovery audit was measuring the wrong thing, three ways** (found in review with Droid GPT-5.6 Terra).
  1. **Tournament bouts are not blade decisions.** Round two of a Major has its format fixed by the bracket; counting it graded the committee's schedule as the stable's judgement. Decisions now filter on `tournamentId === null` — bracket rows still feed the scout history, because a bracket fight is real evidence even when it wasn't a choice.
  2. **The verdict graded raw selected-format hits.** A card lands where the scout said AND where `EXPLORE` sent it AND where the lobby had room, so "hits climbed with age" could be true of a report that taught nobody anything. The verdict now grades the **scout's own top-ranked blade against its own random baseline**.
  3. **The denominator was full of coin flips.** The population's home-blade margins measured **p10 1.9 / p50 11.1 / p90 28.3** weighted stat points — *half the flock had no home blade worth finding*, and grading a scout on those birds reports noise as failure. New `DOCTOR.DISCOVERY_HOME_MARGIN` (10) prints a clear-home line beside the raw one: on the same logs, **31.2% → 35.6% (≥10) → 47.6% (≥25)** against an unmoved 20% baseline. The scout was never as blind as the number said. Both denominators print, because the gap between them is the population-shape story and it belongs in the report rather than hidden by it.
- **THE THREE BREEDING SHAPES (Zane's ruling).** `BREEDING_SHAPES` — the ADJACENT pairs on the distance dial: **agility & sight, sight & stamina, stamina & gameness** — derived from `DISTANCE_STATS` order, never listed. A line aims at a pair FIRST, then takes station, condition and element stars where it can. Why adjacent pairs: the round-28a `pairs` case measured it — a pair is strong at both blades its stats key, still ahead of a flat bird in the middle, and **never below ~62% at any blade where a single spike drops to ~55% at the blade it guessed wrong**. Under the fog, that floor is what a breeder actually owns. Single spikes remain a legitimate, narrower line.
- **The bots stop shuffling and start shopping** (`BREEDING_PLAN`, `foalScore`). `playFarm` took the first legal cover off a shuffled list — the same door-with-no-appetite failure as claiming in round 19 and paid gacha in round 22, and the direct cause of the flat flock above. Every barn now carries a `housePair`, prices every legal (hen, stud) pair on the expected foal (the parents' midpoint — public arithmetic a player can do, since both parents are retired) and buys the best, falling to its second choice when a cover is refused. Four terms: **shape 0.6 / level 0.5 / anchors 0.25 / stars 15 per half-star.** The shape weight deliberately *inverts* the measured win-rate ratio (a level point is worth ~0.30 win points against a separation point's ~0.18) by about 3×, because the two terms are not under equal pressure: level rises on its own as every barn stands its best retirees at stud, while **nothing pushes back on shape drifting to zero**. An unbiased plan leaves the flock exactly where round 29 found it. Star weight is *low* on purpose — the wheel is symmetric (one element in five is prey, one is predator), so a big star rating is near EV-neutral except that it can be TIMED. House shapes are spread UNEVENLY across the roster: an equal split would make every shape equally cheap forever, and scarcity is a price signal.
- `StudView` gains `halfStars` (the number beside the "2.5★ Fire" string) — stars are the wheel's volume knob, so a shopper comparing bloodlines wants arithmetic, not a parse. The Handbook gains a **"What to breed for"** section and the MCP instructions gain the shape doctrine, both computed from `BREEDING_SHAPES`.
- Still deferred: `GHOST_PACE` recalibration (above), Crowd Noise, logarithmic breeding speed, and the matched multi-seed current-vs-end-first discovery policy comparison — which was **not run this round on purpose**, because comparing two policies through a broken audit would have measured the audit.

**Round 30** (2026-08-06) — **the Pit Figure gets a UNIT, and the flock gets a pedigree.** Round 29 deferred "recalibrate `GHOST_PACE`" as its own round. This is that round, and the recalibration turned out to be the wrong fix: the scale did not need moving, it needed *anchoring*.
- **THE PIT FIGURE REBUILT — spine × night.** Zane's diagnosis (2026-08-06): "Going in the direction of DPS vs. a fixed target dummy was the right call, but somehow better defining what it's supposed to be should help us design and tune it." The missing piece was the UNIT. In PFL a figure is anchored to seconds at 8 furlongs, and that one physical fact means a grade step is a fixed number of points forever and the scale *cannot* drift. Rounds 20–29 only looked like that: `pace / GHOST_PACE × 100` reads as "percent of a maxed bird", but `pace` was wind DEALT, which depends on who you fought — so it was never measured against a fixed thing. Two proofs it wasn't: it **drifted** (round 27 rescaled the wind and every figure in the game moved ~20 points, silently), and **we had already patched around it** (`CLASS_BASE`/`CLASS_DIVISOR` existed only because pace lost the quality signal — turns roll on the DIFFERENCE between two books, so two maxed birds trade exactly as much wind as two starters). The ghost was never a bird; it was a divisor named after one. The rebuild: the **SPINE** is the bird's weighted stat blend at that blade on a fixed scale, dice-free and opponent-free; the **NIGHT** is the ratio of the roll bonus it actually rolled to what a `NOMINAL_CONDITION` bird would have rolled (condition, the element wheel, the day's weather, station's clawback and the fuel wall all land here); a loss is marked down by beaten lengths as a **SHARE** rather than a flat subtraction; one shared track variant fogs both sides so the fog never reorders the result. **Deleted:** `GHOST_PACE`, `GHOST_FIGURE`, `CLASS_BASE`, `CLASS_DIVISOR`, `BEATEN_SCALE`, `MIN_BEATEN` and `MAX` — the last on Zane's ruling, "let's forget about the 0–150 range, I don't think it's helpful to cap pit figures like this." A bred monster posting 140 should read 140.
- **Two anchors that turned out to be the same anchor.** Offered "a flat 1000 bird at its best blade posts 100" or "the grade step is the peg", Zane chose the first — and because the spine is LINEAR in the blend, both are true at once: `PEG_STAT` 1000 = `PEG_FIGURE` 100 means a letter grade (100 stat points, `grades.ts`) is worth exactly **10 figure points at every rung of the ladder**. The PFL analogy — a grade is a fixed 1.0 seconds — stopped being aspirational and became literal.
- **The happy accident: blade fit was already multiplicative and nobody had noticed.** Every format's weight matrix sums to exactly 1.00 (round 27's symmetry audit), so a FLAT bird blends identically at all five blades — cross-blade comparability *by construction*, with no hand-tuned per-blade table, which is precisely the job `GHOST_PACE` had been doing by hand. And a SHAPED bird's fit falls out proportionally with no fit term written anywhere. Zane asked for a ~25% multiplicative swing; the weights already delivered it. Measured on a true specialist (pair +200, off-pair −200), home−middle across five levels: **11.2 / 11.6 / 10.5 / 9.6 / 7.5** at base 320/500/800/1200/1600 — near-flat, where round 29 measured the old additive fit *collapsing* 11.9 → 3.5 as birds improved. Home−worst GROWS with the bird: 23.8 → 32.0. The better the bird, the louder its shape. Full table in the `FIGURE` block.
- **The same lesson, learnt twice, now fixed structurally.** Rebuilding the figure moved the scale under the scout — exactly what round 27 did and round 29 caught. Both scout constants were fitted to the figure's OUTPUT, so both went stale:
  - **`SCOUT.OWN_GRADE_STEP` 15 → 10**, and it is no longer fitted at all — it is **derived** from the figure's own construction (100 stat points / `PEG_STAT` × `PEG_FIGURE`). Measurement now confirms rather than sets it: B 21.4 → B+ 27.1 → A 37.1 over 5,588 fights. `src/engine/scout.test.ts` **pins the derivation**, so a future re-peg of `PEG_STAT`/`PEG_FIGURE` fails loudly instead of quietly leaving the scout 18 points adrift.
  - **`SCOUT.OPPONENT_GRADE_STEP` 5 → 0.** The opponent has left the figure entirely — the spine reads the bird's own blend, so there is nothing of the opponent to remove. Measured, holding own grade at B+: 27.0 against B+ company, 27.8 against B, 25.8 against A — flat inside the noise. Kept as a knob at zero rather than deleted, because beaten lengths still carry the opponent faintly and a future rule that pays for company would want the dial back.
  - **`SCOUT.PRIOR_FIGURE` 30 → 27** (the measured B+ mean). This one genuinely **cannot** be derived, and the reason is worth recording: the spine says a B+ bird should figure ~35, but the live flock averages 27 because its CONDITION is starter-grade (~320 against a nominal 1000), so nearly every bird fights below nominal form and every loser is marked down on top. Those are properties of the POPULATION, not the formula — so this number will drift UP on its own as the flock breeds up. Re-measure it when the BLOODLINES ladder shows the mean grade has moved a band; do not fit it to a formula it does not obey.
  - `src/engine/balance/cases.ts`: `FIGURE_GRADE_STEP` 5 → derived (10), with a 10% tolerance for measurement quantization, and `GRADE_BAND` is now exported from `src/engine/grades.ts` so nothing re-types the literal 100.
- **The payoff, measured.** Fixing the scout's calibration moved discovery hard. On the "clear home" verdict (birds whose home blade actually beats its runner-up — the scout is not graded on coin flips), **age 4+ went 30.5% → 57.5%** against a 20% random baseline, 83.5% on-or-adjacent; the fresh round-30 world reads clear-home **57.3% (age 1) / 50.9% (age 2–3) / 57.5% (age 4+)** and raw accuracy 50.4 / 41.8 / 44.0. The balance suite's grade-calibration table now reads +10.1 / +9.9 / +9.7 / +11.5 / +11.6 / +11.7 / +11.5 / +11.6 / +12.2 / +10.9 / +10.8 / +10.8 / +10.7 / +10.7 / +10.4 against the **derived** +10 target — the engine keeping a promise its config makes, rather than hitting a number somebody chose.
- **GENERATION MARKER (Zane's ruling).** Starters and gacha pulls are generation 0; a chick takes its **dam's** generation + 1. New `birds.generation` column (`schema.ts` + `ddl.ts` hand-synced; worlds are remade, not migrated), **un-fogged** on `BirdView` — a generation is pedigree, not shape, so it says nothing round 28's fog protects — surfaced on MCP `get_bird`/`list_flock`, and a new **BLOODLINES** doctor section reporting per generation: count, mean grade, mean stars and median home-blade margin. The margin uses the IDENTICAL `homeBlade` arithmetic the scout audit is graded on, so "better tuned" means the same thing in both places. Latest 91-day sim, gen 0 → 1 → 2: **328.2 → 335.7 → 348.1** mean stat (+20.0), 1.75★ → 1.74★ → 2.06★, home margin 8.1 → 11.2 → 10.3. The loop compounds — slowly. Until this section existed there was no way to see whether it compounded at all.
- **HENS ARE BRED TO THEIR OWN SHAPE.** Zane: "Each hen is different, and ought to be bred strategically. If a hen has b1/b2 oriented stats, it should breed with a b1/b2 oriented rooster." Round 29 aimed every cover in a barn at ONE house axis, so roughly two hens in three were bred *across* their own grain and the foal's midpoint undid the dam instead of compounding her. The shape is now picked PER HEN off her own sheet (new `bestShape` helper), with **`BREEDING_PLAN.OWN_SHAPE_MIN` = 40** as the bar below which she falls back to the barn's axis. Measured, not felt: over all 149 breeding hens in the round-29 sim, best-pair separation ran p25 = 32, median = 55.5, p75 = 91 — so the genuinely shapeless third falls back and the rest is bred to itself. `housePair` was **demoted to a fallback rather than deleted**: a near-flat hen following her own noise is following nothing, and scattering a barn's covers across all three axes destroys the concentration that made round 29's uneven house spread a price signal.
- **The doctor's breeding ruler had to follow the ruling.** Round 29's adoption line measured every cover against the BARN's axis. Once hens were bred to their own grain that ruler was measuring a policy the bots had stopped following — it read dams at −9.0 and made a working plan look broken. Rebuilt to measure the **choice**: for each cover, how far the chosen SIRE sits along the DAM's own shape, against the floor of what an *unchosen* sire would score on those same axes. Latest sim: **sires chosen +67.0 against an unchosen sire +1.0**, foals landing at +59.5. Nothing but selection produces that gap. An honesty note worth keeping: the first version of the accompanying test asserted that covered hens carry MORE shape than the average bird. It passed on a 20-day world and is FALSE in a mature one (hens +59.0 vs any bird +64.5) — because the flock number is lifted by the plan's own foals while the breeding hens are mostly unselected founders. The assertion was **removed rather than tuned**.
- **Two footguns fixed, both found by being bitten.** (a) `latestSimDb()` picked the newest sim by NAME, so a hand-named keeper (`sim-20260806-season-c.db`) sorted after `sim-20260806-1636.db` and the doctor reported on a world three schema versions old; it now orders by **mtime**. (b) Worse, it did so SILENTLY — SQLite renders a double-quoted unknown identifier as a string literal, so the newly added `generation` column printed as the literal string `"generation"` for all 530 birds without a single error. `scripts/doctor.ts` now **refuses** a world whose schema predates the columns it needs, and says what to do about it.
- Still deferred, unchanged from round 29: Crowd Noise (station's per-fight stage level), logarithmic breeding speed + the years-to-max analysis, the B1 +200 spec retarget, and the matched multi-seed current-vs-end-first discovery policy comparison. **Newly opened:** Element in the breeding score — a cross-element cover should cost star potential so bots keep lines pure. Zane deferred it, suspecting the population is too thin and the star incentive too weak for it to measure yet.

**Round 31** (2026-08-06, two commits) — **THE CARD: fights are POSTED now, not conjured.** Round 30 left the rising unmatched rate as an open hypothesis blaming the scout. It was not the scout. `Lobbies.enter` created the lobby when the key didn't exist, so every fight type was available every day and the perfect fight always existed *because you invented it by asking*. Zane: "The on-demand lobbies of perfect fights is crazy lol. Never intended that." Measured over 91 days: **74 live keys taking ~70 entries a day, 2.9 birds per lobby against a capacity of 8, and 16.3% of entries never drawing an opponent** — decomposing as 35% sole entrant, 31% two barn-mates alone (matchmaking never pairs same-barn birds), 34% odd bird out. The first two are pure key-space damage; no matchmaker can fix them, only collision can.
- **THE KEY SPACE CUT 75 → 50, on three rulings.** (a) **Hardcore leaves the daily card entirely** — Zane: "There should be 0 hardcore fights outside the Finals. This is a regression in the fight schedule." It had already measured its own case: 201 entries producing 55 fights at a **45.3% unmatched rate**, the worst of any mode, for under one fight a day. `FIGHT_MODES` is now `juvenile | real` and lives in config (it had been a bare union in `lobbies.ts`, re-typed literally in four other places); hardcore survives only in the Pintakasi Majors, which are tournaments and never open a lobby, so `battleLog.mode` still carries "hardcore" for those. (b) **`nw2` merged into `nw3`** — of 181 active birds, nw2's exclusive constituency was **10 birds** and nw3's **18**: two classes and ten keys separating 28 birds. One conditioned rung now serves all 148 birds under three wins (`NW_CAP`). (c) **Claimer tags thinned**, `PRICES` 5 rungs → `[50, 200, 600]` and `JUVENILE_PRICES` 3 → `[25, 100]`. This was the single biggest fragmentation fix available anywhere: the tag is part of the key, so claimers alone were **40 of the 75 keys — 53% of the whole space off one axis** — taking 13.1 entries a day divided into **0.33 entries per key**. The dear rungs were not thin, they were dead: b3@400 drew TWO entries in 84 days.
- **A PUBLISHED DAILY CARD** (`CARD`, `cardOfDay`, `isOnCard`). ~11 keys a day, 10 on the Majors' crown day. Pure function of the day index, exactly like `weatherOfDay` — no schema, no storage, reproducible for any past day, and `cardOfDay(day + 1)` is free, so **tomorrow is public and plannable**. The PFL rhythm Zane asked for: "if you just wanted to wait for a perfect race, you might have to wait 3-4 days, this causes players to settle for close-to-ideal races." **Where the scarcity goes is the whole design**: every CLASS runs every day in both divisions and what rotates is which BLADES each class runs. Two reasons — the classes NEST (maiden ⊂ nw3 ⊂ open) and 33 of 181 active birds are open-only, so adult open must be posted daily or veterans strand (nothing may ever be stranded); and the blade is the DISCOVERY axis, so the shortage lands on the most interesting question a player has.
- **The rotating deck, and the two bugs it had.** Blades come from a deterministically shuffled deck walked with a per-class stride, so every blade is *guaranteed* once per deck rather than left to independent draws. (a) A slot group straddling a deck boundary drew from two independently shuffled decks and could pick the same blade twice, silently costing that day a lobby — fixed by skipping a blade already posted. (b) Thinning adult open on crown day by asking the deck for 2 blades instead of 3 changed the cursor stride and **desynced the rotation**, measured as the worst blade gap jumping to 5 — fixed by always walking the full count and slicing after (`take`). Measured gaps after: k=3 and k=2 classes reach every blade within **4 days**, k=1 classes within **8**, and the whole card within **3 days** in both divisions.
- **THE CHOOSER INVERTED — blade first, class as the slack.** The load-bearing design change, and it came out of that k=1 gap. The old `pickSpec` picked a class off the bird's record and then a blade; ported naively that starves a class running one blade a day, and **a juvenile's whole discovery year is 7 days** against an 8-day worst gap — a winless chick could have finished its career without ever seeing a blade in a maiden. Inverting uses the nesting: find the blade among everything the bird is *legally allowed to enter today*, then take the most PROTECTIVE class posted at that blade. A winless juvenile whose blade isn't in today's maiden simply runs juvenile open at that blade. **Maiden becomes protection you get when the card offers it**, and the whole-card gap (3 days) governs coverage instead of the per-class one.
- **ONE PREDICATE, TWO CALLERS** — `entryRefusal(bird, spec)` extracted, returning the reason or null. `Lobbies.enter` throws it; the chooser filters on it. The ladder had already been encoded twice (there and in `ladderClass`), and a third copy inside the chooser would have been the drift that kills: **both entry paths wrap `enter` in `quietly()`**, so a chooser proposing specs the door rejects would surface as nothing at all except a fill rate that silently collapsed. The card door is a BACKSTOP; the choosers are the mechanism.
- **UNBOUNDED LOBBIES** (Zane: "no more 8-max and then duplicating the lobby. Just let it grow infinitely."). `LOBBY.CAPACITY` and the `capacity` column deleted; exactly ONE lobby per posted key per day. Capacity-8 duplication was splitting a hot key straight back into two half-empty rooms — undoing what the card exists to do, in the same breath. It also removes any reason to CAMP: under a cap a late entrant could find the room full and be shunted into a duplicate with a different field, so watching and waiting paid; unbounded, you can always get in and the fill count you saw only grows. Two consequences recorded honestly: the round-17 per-farm seating cap **died with its denominator** (it was `capacity/2`) and was dropped rather than replaced — a barn that pours its roster into one key strands its own surplus, refunded in full, and the doctor watches same-barn stranding so we find out rather than assume; and **8 was EVEN on purpose**, so unbounded means parity is a coin flip and a lobby that closes odd still strands one bird.
- **`findOrOpenLobby` now MATCHES on `dayOpened`**, which it had always written and never read. Harmless only because `resolve` closes every open lobby each tick — the moment anything closes selectively (the real-time clock closes claimers early) a stale lobby could have seated tomorrow's entrants on a key that is no longer posted. The card has to be authoritative, so the day is part of the lookup.
- **`POINTS_FOR.hardcore` is now UNREACHABLE**, and every sentence teaching "or two hardcore wins" was already false. Crown points are written only from a lobby fight (`POINTS_FOR[lobby.mode]`) and tournament wins bank none, so the only route to `QUALIFYING_POINTS` is three real wins. Fixed across the wiki and the MCP prose. The fields **survived** losing the route: majors 29 run / 1 cancelled at a field of **11.0** (round 30: 9.6).
- **A PRE-EXISTING BUG FOUND AND FIXED FIRST, in its own commit (`d662d72`).** The crown-day door gated on `isCrownDay` (Thursday) but queried pending tournament entries **without filtering by division** — the division lives on `tournaments`, not the entry. So on WEDNESDAY a Juvenile Championship registrant was never blocked: it entered a normal lobby AND fought its crown the same day. Nothing caught it — the tournament stamps its `battleLog` rows with Thursday's day index so `checkFightCap` never saw both, and the doctor's one-card-per-bird-per-day invariant buckets on `lobbyEntries` only. Landed separately on purpose, so an inflated juvenile fight count wasn't read as the schedule change moving numbers.
- **THE RESULT, on a fresh 91-day world.** Mean birds per lobby **2.9 → 7.36**; entries never drawing an opponent **16.3% → 4.5%**; lobbies holding a single bird **334 (16.6%) → 26 (3.9%)**; same-barn-only stranding **~302 birds → 25**; lobbies 2,012 → 673. All five invariants pass and **0 health warnings** (round 30 ended on 2). Claimer keys went from 0.33 entries per key to roughly **4.4** — the marketplace got healthier, not smaller — with `claims placed` adoption holding at 14/15 farms.

New `src/engine/card.test.ts`: 16 tests, ~11,700 assertions, including an exhaustive proof that **no shape of bird is ever stranded** on any day (enumerated over age × stakes record × juvenile record) and that eggs and retired birds are refused everywhere.

Only a room holding a single entry would strand anyone.
